## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative yet forward-thinking aesthetic for advanced fiscal education and corporate taxation. The brand reconciles the high-stakes rigor of fiscal compliance with the fluid, luminous modernity of high-tier fintech and contemporary editorial layouts.

The visual narrative relies on a deliberate balance of contrasts:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep Midnight backgrounds (`#0F1122`) are deployed to frame high-impact hero moments, complex curriculum hubs, and immersive data modules, while Glacial Blue (`#EFF6FC`) and crisp white panels preserve cognitive clarity and reading ease across long-form modules.
- **Architectural Authority:** Sharp grid structures, asymmetrical Bento layouts, and layered floating elements inspire trust, precision, and executive pedigree.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Cyan and Electric Blue accents highlight interactive triggers, progression bars, and live cohort statuses, accented by warm gold micro-details for prestige signifiers.

The experience addresses corporate tax advisors, financial directors, and legal professionals who seek rigorous knowledge delivery packaged with clean, tech-forward sophistication.

## Colors

The palette synthesizes institutional confidence with digital-first vibrancy across layered backgrounds and content containers:

- **Primary (`#376AAC`):** The structural core. Drives institutional trust, major section headers, foundational brand elements, and primary callouts.
- **Secondary (`#26BBE4`):** Electric Cyan provides interactive energy, indicator dots, active states, progress metrics, and fine edge glints.
- **Tertiary (`#0F1122`):** Midnight Navy acts as a deep backdrop for high-contrast hero segments, executive banners, dark Bento modules, and high-fidelity code/tax law containers.
- **Neutral (`#EFF6FC`):** Glacial Blue canvas tint designed to eliminate stark contrast fatigue while remaining crisp, airy, and clinical.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - *Accent Gold (`#F1D649`):* Micro-badges, certification seals, premium tags, and key takeaways.
  - *Success / WhatsApp (`#40A677`):* Direct advisory triggers, real-time enrollment alerts, verified status chips, and official messaging pathways.
  - *Surface White (`#FFFFFF`):* Elevates foreground cards, reading surfaces, and interactive form controls.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites editorial impact with technical legibility:

- **Display & Section Headers (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Tuned for authoritative presence. The wide aperture, geometric build, and tightened tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) in the larger scales give executive headlines a crisp, modern delivery.
- **Editorial & Analytical Body (Inter):** Serves all legal texts, complex taxation regulations, data tables, and navigation. Inter provides high x-height readability, distinct numeric glyphs, and neutral rendering across high-density layouts.
- **Uppercase Metadata (Inter):** Small labels, accreditation badges, and taxonomy pills utilize increased letter spacing (+0.05em) and 700 weight for immediate legibility at glance value.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an asymmetric Bento-box philosophy grounded by a 12-column grid:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width bounded at 1440px with 4rem outer margins and 2rem gutters. Bento cards scale rhythmically across spans of 4, 6, 8, and 12 columns to balance dense analytical data with spacious typographic pull quotes.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** Adapts to an 8-column system with 2rem margins. Complex bento configurations collapse into balanced 4-column pairs or full-width cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Reflows to a strict 4-column layout with 1rem lateral safe zones. Horizontal scroll snaps are utilized for interactive course matrices and comparison tiers to preserve screen economy.
- **Section Spacing Rhythm:** Alternates between airy 5rem–6rem module transitions and dense internal card spacing (`space-md` to `space-xl`) to articulate categorical grouping without artificial dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with physical layering, subtle refraction, and controlled luminescence rather than conventional heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Glacial Blue (`#EFF6FC`) in light modules; deep Midnight Navy (`#0F1122`) in specialized immersive zones.
- **Glassmorphic Bento Containers:** Pure white or midnight surfaces set to 85%–92% opacity, backed by a `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur. Edges are framed with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` on light, or `1px solid rgba(38, 187, 228, 0.15)` on dark) to simulate bevel-cut glass.
- **Floating 3D Assets & Overlays:** Interactive summaries and preview tokens employ an ambient multi-stop shadow:
  - Ambient base: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(15, 17, 34, 0.08)`
  - Specular lift: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(55, 106, 172, 0.14)`
- **Luminescent Accent Glows:** Active indicators and call-to-actions project a soft cyan halo (`0 0 20px rgba(38, 187, 228, 0.35)`), creating the sensation of back-lit digital hardware.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced, refined contour language:

- **Bento & Master Containers:** Feature substantial corner radii (1.25rem to 1.5rem / 20px–24px), establishing clean framing around dense educational materials while preserving modern organic geometry.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** Standardized on `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons and inputs, and `1rem` (16px) for nested sub-cards.
- **Status Pills & Micro-Chips:** Full pill shapes (`9999px`) provide contrast against the squared-off structural Bento modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Corporate Blue (`#376AAC`) background, pure white typography, 0.5rem border radius, and subtle specular top-border highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). Hover triggers a transition to an Electric Cyan edge illumination.
- **Direct Advisory (WhatsApp):** Emerald Green (`#40A677`) fill, white bold typography, paired with an integrated SVG logo and a soft radial green shadow for instant distinction.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Glacial Blue background with 1px border of `#376AAC` at 25% opacity, shifting to solid `#376AAC` typography.

### Bento Cards & Floating Modules
- **Editorial Layout:** Asymmetric cards featuring a hierarchy of data: large numerical counters in Plus Jakarta Sans 48px, followed by 14px Inter descriptive captions.
- **Dark Mode Feature Bento:** Deep `#0F1122` card body with hairline Electric Cyan border glow, containing high-relief typography and floating preview pills.

### Chips & Badges
- **Accreditation Badge:** Pale Yellow (`#F1D649` at 15% opacity) with dark amber typography (`#7C6300`) and a 0.5rem radius, denoting official degrees and tax reforms.
- **Status Indicator:** Pill container with an active, pulsing 6px dot in `#26BBE4` (Live / Enrolling) or `#40A677` (Verified).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Fields:** Pure white surface, 1px neutral border (`#D5E4F3`), 0.5rem corner radius, and 48px height. On focus: transitions to a 1.5px `#26BBE4` ring with `0 0 0 4px rgba(38, 187, 228, 0.15)`.
- **Labels:** Inter 13px weight 600 in deep navy, positioned with 6px vertical spacing above the field.

### Curriculum Accordion & Lists
- Surface white containers with hairline divider lines (`#EFF6FC`). Active module items showcase an Electric Cyan left indicator strip (3px wide) and a smooth background fade into Glacial Blue.
